---
title: Token Factory
updatedAt: "2026-10-12"
---

The TokenFactory module lets users and contracts create native bank denoms of the form
`factory/{creator address}/{subdenom}`, without deploying a contract. Representing assets as native
bank denoms is what gives them access to the rest of the chain: exchange, auction, insurance funds and
permissions all operate on bank denoms.
