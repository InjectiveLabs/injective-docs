Cypher OS is a private-transfer wallet for assets on Injective.
You move tokens from your regular wallet into a shielded balance, send them to other people without revealing the amount or who received them, and move them back out whenever you want.

This guide walks through the public testnet at [`testnet.cypheros.injective.tech`](https://testnet.cypheros.injective.tech/).

<Warning>
  Cypher OS runs on Injective testnet only.
  Testnet tokens have no value, and the testnet may be reset or redeployed, which can clear your shielded balance.
  Don't send real funds to any address you see in the app.
</Warning>

## How it works

Using Cypher OS has three steps:

1. **Shield**: move tokens from your regular wallet into your shielded balance. This transaction is public.
2. **Send privately**: send shielded tokens to another person's Private Transfer Address. The amount and the recipient stay private.
3. **Unshield**: move tokens from your shielded balance back to a regular wallet address. This transaction is public.

The testnet supports two assets:

| Asset | What backs it on testnet |
|---|---|
| INJ | [Wrapped INJ (wINJ)](/developers-evm/wrapped-inj), converted from your INJ automatically when you shield |
| USDC | [Testnet USD Coin (USDC)](/developers-defi/usdc-stablecoin) |

## Before you start

You need:

- **A browser wallet for Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) networks**, such as MetaMask.
  Use a regular account.
  Smart-contract wallets and some hardware or multi-party wallets aren't supported, because Cypher OS needs your wallet to produce the same signature every time (see [Your Private Transfer Address](#your-private-transfer-address)).
- **Testnet INJ** from the [Injective testnet faucet](https://testnet.faucet.injective.network/).
  You need some INJ for gas even if you only plan to use USDC.
- **Testnet USDC** (optional) from the [Circle faucet](https://faucet.circle.com/), on Injective testnet.

You don't need to add the network yourself.
When you connect, Cypher OS asks your wallet to add or switch to Injective testnet (EVM chain ID `1439`).

## Step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Open [`testnet.cypheros.injective.tech`](https://testnet.cypheros.injective.tech/) and select **Connect wallet**.
    Approve the network switch if your wallet asks.

    While the page loads, Cypher OS downloads the proof files it needs to generate proofs in your browser.
    Shield and Send Privately become available when the download finishes.

    The first time you connect, your wallet asks you to sign a message that starts with "This is onboarding to zeto".
    It asks twice: Cypher OS checks that both signatures match before it creates your Private Transfer Address.
    Signing is free and doesn't send a transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shield tokens">
    Select **Shield**, choose INJ or USDC, and enter an amount.
    Your wallet asks you to confirm up to three transactions:

    1. **Convert INJ to wINJ**, only when you shield INJ and your wINJ balance doesn't cover the amount.
    2. **Approve the token**, once per asset, when the current approval doesn't cover the amount.
    3. **Shield**, which moves the tokens into your shielded balance.

    Before the shield transaction, the app generates a zero-knowledge (ZK) proof in your browser.

    When you shield INJ, **Max** keeps 0.01 INJ in your wallet for gas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a Private Transfer Address">
    To receive private transfers, share your Private Transfer Address.
    Open your account menu, select **Private Transfer Address**, then reveal and copy it.

    A Private Transfer Address is not a wallet address.
    It's a 64-character code that only Cypher OS uses, and it's safe to share.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send privately">
    Select **Send Privately**, choose the asset, and enter the amount and the recipient's Private Transfer Address.

    You don't sign anything in your wallet and you don't pay gas.
    The app proves the transfer in your browser, then a relayer submits it to Injective and pays the gas.
    The recipient's shielded balance updates once the transfer is confirmed.

    Your shielded balance is made of separate notes, one for each shield or incoming transfer.
    One transfer can spend at most two of them.
    If you shielded in many small amounts, the app may tell you the most you can send in a single transfer.
    Send that amount or less.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unshield">
    Open an asset and select **Unshield**.
    Enter an amount and choose the recipient:

    - **This wallet** sends the tokens to the wallet you connected.
    - **Another wallet** sends them to any EVM address you enter. Check the address carefully, because you can't reverse an unshield.

    Like a private send, an unshield goes through the relayer, so you don't sign or pay gas.
    A large amount may be sent in several transactions.

    Unshielding INJ pays out wINJ, which the app counts toward your INJ balance.
    To use it as INJ in other apps, unwrap it as described in [Wrapped INJ (wINJ)](/developers-evm/wrapped-inj).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What other people can see

| Action | Visible to everyone on Injective | Private |
|---|---|---|
| Shield | Your wallet address, the asset, and the amount | Your Private Transfer Address |
| Send privately | That a private transfer happened, and when | The sender, the recipient, and the amount |
| Unshield | The recipient address, the asset, and the amount | Which shielded balance the tokens came from |

Shielding and unshielding are the public edges of Cypher OS.
If you shield an unusual amount and unshield the same amount soon after, an observer can guess that the two are connected.
Private transfers hide more when you wait between steps and avoid round or unique amounts.

## Your Private Transfer Address

Cypher OS derives your private key from the signature you gave when you first connected.
It doesn't store your key on a server, and nobody else, including Injective Labs, can see it.

- **On another device or browser**, connect the same wallet and sign the same message again.
  You get the same Private Transfer Address and the same shielded balance.
- **Only sign the "This is onboarding to zeto" message on Cypher OS.**
  Anyone who gets that signature can spend your shielded balance.
- **Your browser caches your key** so that you don't sign every visit.
  Disconnecting doesn't clear it.
  Clear the site data in your browser to remove it.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The app says my proof files are preparing">
    Cypher OS downloads its proof files when the page loads.
    Keep the tab open until the download finishes, and select **View progress** to follow it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My balance didn't update after a transfer">
    A transfer reaches Injective before it's confirmed, and your balance updates on its own a few seconds later.
    If it still hasn't changed after a minute, reload the page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My wallet was refused during setup">
    Cypher OS refuses wallets whose two setup signatures don't match, because the Private Transfer Address would change between sessions and you could lose access to your shielded balance.
    Use a regular wallet account instead of a smart-contract or multi-party wallet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I unshielded INJ but see wINJ in my wallet">
    Unshielding pays out wrapped INJ (wINJ).
    See [Wrapped INJ (wINJ)](/developers-evm/wrapped-inj) to convert it back to INJ.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
