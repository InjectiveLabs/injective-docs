An index perpetual is a futures contract that tracks an index, such as a basket of assets or a benchmark. The SICI/USDC perpetual on Injective tracks the [Super Intelligence Compute Index](https://injective.com/blog/super-intelligence-compute-index), a benchmark for the cost of renting five NVIDIA GPU models.

Index perpetuals have no preset expiry. Traders can go long or short, and periodic funding payments help keep the contract price close to the index. The exchange uses the mark price to value positions and determine when they become eligible for liquidation. SICI/USDC uses USDC as margin.

### Mark Price Mechanism

LO:TECH publishes an hourly SICI fixing based on the trailing seven-day average of a weighted basket of GPU rental prices. An index level of 113 means the benchmark is 13% above its base level of 100.

The Provider oracle relays the latest fixing onchain via SEDA Fast and converts it from USD to USDC for the perpetual's mark price. For example, if the fixing is 113 and 1 USDC is worth $1, the mark price is 113 USDC. The index input stays at the latest fixing until LO:TECH publishes the next one. See [Oracle Feed Sources](/defi/trading/oracle-feed-sources#compute) for the basket weights and market ID.
