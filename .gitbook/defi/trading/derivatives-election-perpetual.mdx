An election perpetual is a futures contract that tracks the price of an election outcome on a prediction market.

The contract has no preset expiry. Traders can go long or short, and periodic funding payments help keep the contract price close to the reference price. The exchange uses the mark price to value positions and determine when they become eligible for liquidation.

See the [equity perpetual mark-price example](/defi/trading/derivatives-iassets#mark-price-example) for a worked calculation.

### Market Settlement

The prediction market resolves the election outcome under its own rules. After the outcome is final, an Injective governance proposal can specify how to settle the perpetual and close any remaining positions.
