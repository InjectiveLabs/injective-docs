Liquidation reduces or closes derivative exposure when its supporting equity falls below the maintenance requirement. Initial margin determines how much collateral is required to open exposure. Maintenance margin determines when existing exposure becomes liquidatable.

## Isolated liquidation

An isolated position becomes eligible for liquidation when its equity falls below the maintenance requirement. Ignoring funding and fees, let `Q` be the positive position quantity, `P` the entry price, `M` the mark price, `m` the assigned margin, and `r` the maintenance margin ratio. The margin needed to stay at or above maintenance is:

```text
maintenance requirement = Q × M × r
long:  m ≥ maintenance requirement − Q × (M − P)
short: m ≥ maintenance requirement − Q × (P − M)
```

For a long of 1 BTC entered at 100,000 USDC with 10,000 USDC of assigned margin and a 5% maintenance ratio, the maintenance requirement starts at 5,000 USDC. When the mark price falls to 94,000, the 6,000 USDC unrealized loss leaves 4,000 USDC of equity. Required maintenance falls to 4,700 USDC, so the position becomes eligible for liquidation. Include funding and fees when calculating actual equity.

### How isolated liquidation works

1. A liquidation can close the position by selling a long or buying back a short. Execution follows the protocol's price bounds and depends on available liquidity.
2. The exchange accounts for realized gains or losses, funding, and applicable fees when closing the position.
3. The protocol distributes any liquidation surplus according to its payout rules, including the liquidator reward. Liquidation can consume the entire margin assigned to a position.

See [Performing Liquidations](/defi/trading/margin-performing-liquidations) for the execution details.

## Cross margin liquidation

Where cross margin is enabled, a pool can become liquidatable when its equity falls below the combined maintenance requirement of its eligible positions. The liquidation process can first cancel eligible open orders and release their holds. If the pool still needs a close, a permitted partial liquidation can reduce exposure while leaving some positions open. During the cooldown window, the pool can qualify for another partial close while it is above bare maintenance and below its configured recovery target.

For example, after a partial close a pool with 820 USDC of equity and 800 USDC of maintenance has cleared bare maintenance. If the recovery target is 840 USDC, the pool is still below that target.

Availability depends on network configuration, market and collateral eligibility, the effective margin mode, and the configured liquidation router. Use the [pool snapshot](/developers-native/query-chain/exchange#check-cross-margin-mode-and-pool-health) to monitor liquidation equity and maintenance for the actual pool.

## Managing liquidation risk

- **Monitor margin.** Track position equity or pool health as mark prices and open orders change.
- **Understand stop orders.** A stop order can reduce exposure after its trigger is reached. Execution depends on liquidity, and liquidation can occur before the stop order fills.
- **Maintain a margin buffer.** More collateral relative to exposure provides a larger buffer against adverse price moves.
- **Account for funding.** Funding payments can reduce position equity even when prices are unchanged. See [Funding Rates](/defi/trading/margin-funding-rates).
