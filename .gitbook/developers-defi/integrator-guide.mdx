## What Every Integrator Gets

| Capability | What it means |
|---|---|
| **MultiVM Token Standard** | One token, one balance across EVM and Native Injective. No wrapping. |
| **Native CLOB** | On-chain orderbook with FBA. Launch a spot market in one transaction. |
| **Sub-second finality** | ~0.65s block time with instant finality. |
| **USDC liquidity** | Native USDC via Circle's CCTP. Already live. |
| **40% fee share** | Exchange dApps earn 40% of trading fees from orders they originate. |
| **Testnet + faucets** | Full testnet with INJ and USDC faucets, EVM and Native explorers. |
